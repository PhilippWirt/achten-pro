Why & How

Why Playfight works —
and how it works.

Why

Real strength arises where contact is possible. Not as competition. Not as performance proof. But as encounter. You don't train against something — you train with someone.

  • Touch with backbone
  • Community instead of performance pressure
  • Precision instead of randomness — you hold your boundaries

How

Safety instead of freestyle: a restriction ladder instead of open dynamics. Less complexity through 4 clear signals. 5-5 breathing as anchor, kitten principle as mindset, body-first as order.

  • Restriction ladder instead of freestyle
  • 4 signals instead of complexity
  • 5-5 breathing as anchor
  • Kitten principle
  • Body-first, not talk-first
